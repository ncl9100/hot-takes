Key findings to remember:
- NYC break-even heat pump COP vs gas boiler is about 5.6 at $225/MWh electricity and $10/MMBtu gas (energy cost only); waste-heat HP beats air-source HP baseline ($64 vs $90/MWh).
- Con Edison Chelsea TEN pilot takes heat from a chilled-water plant serving two data center tenants to NYCHA (372 apartments DHW). Designed, awaiting construction approval. Check if 111 8th Ave is a source.
- Lake Hawkeye: ~150 MW Phase I, ~300 MW full build, closed-loop glycol cooling, no heat reuse plan, organized local opposition; NY EO 62 (July 14, 2026) pauses approvals for 50 MW+ data centers.
- Neither site has public engineering data (IT load, cooling temps); biggest knowledge gap.
